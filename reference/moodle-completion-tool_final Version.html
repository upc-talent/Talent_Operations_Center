Moodle completion rate tool

Upload a Moodle completion export. Get each student's completion rate and the date they finished the final video. Everything runs in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

1. Upload the completion sheets

Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or CSV. Layout: name, email, then each video's status followed by its timestamp column. Working with several platforms? Add one sheet per platform for the same course and they are merged automatically by email.

Drop one or more files here or click to browse. Adding more files merges them with the ones already loaded.
In the file window, hold Ctrl (Cmd on Mac) or Shift to pick several sheets at once.

2. Final video

Video order differs between courses, so pick the video that marks the end of the course. With several sheets, choose a final video for each sheet. A student counts as finished when they complete the final video of any sheet they appear in. The tool remembers your choices by video name and picks them automatically next time.

3. Which statuses count as completed

These are all the different status texts found in the uploaded files. Click one to include or exclude it.

4. Deadline (optional)

Set the date the course had to be finished. Each student is then marked On time, Late, or Overdue based on when they completed the final video. The time defaults to the end of the day.

5. Who completed before a date (optional)

A simple cut-off check, separate from the deadline. Pick a date to see who had completed the course before it, and who had not.

6. Hide emails from the download (optional)

Paste the emails that must not appear in the downloaded Excel file, or upload a sheet that lists them (any column; every email address found is added to the list). Start an entry with @ to hide a whole domain, for example @example.com. Hidden students stay on this page (dimmed) so you can check them, but they are left out of the file. The list is remembered in this browser, so the sheet only needs to be uploaded once.
