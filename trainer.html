Moodle Reports

Upload Moodle completion exports for a tracked course, check the result, then publish it. The files are read in this browser; only the computed completion rows are saved when you press Publish.

0. Course

+ Track a new course

1. Upload the completion sheets

Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or CSV. Layout: name, email, then each video's status followed by its timestamp column. Several platforms for the same course? Add one sheet per platform — they are merged by email.

Drop one or more files here or click to browse. Adding files merges them with the ones already loaded.

3. Final video

Pick the video that marks the end of the course — one per sheet. A student counts as finished when they complete the final video of any sheet they appear in. Your choices are saved to the course when you publish.

4. Which statuses count as completed

Every status text found in the files. Click one to include or exclude it.

Videos included in the rate ()

5. Deadline (optional)

The date the course had to be finished, in KSA time (UTC+3). The minute is inclusive (23:59 means up to 23:59:59). Each student is then On time, Late or Overdue by when they completed the final video. Saved to the course when you publish.

6. Who completed before a date (optional)

A cut-off check for this session only — shown here and in the local Excel, never published.

7. Leave people out of the publish (optional)

Emails, or @domain.com for a whole domain. They stay visible here (dimmed) but are left out of the publish and the Excel. This list is not saved anywhere — it is cleared when the page reloads or the course changes.

8. Onboarding learners — people not in the roster yet, for completion reports only

They never appear in the supervisor page, Attendance, the Master Sheet or Analytics. If the same email is later added to the roster, the roster record is used. Shared by every course.
